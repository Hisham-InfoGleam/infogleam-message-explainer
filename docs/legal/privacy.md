# Privacy

- The tool runs fully in your web browser.
- The messages you paste are not sent to any server. They are not stored.
  When you close or reload the page, they are gone.
- The page is technically blocked from sending data. A Content Security
  Policy tells the browser that the page may not connect to any server.
- The page loads no fonts, scripts or images from other sites. It has no
  analytics and no tracking.
- The copy buttons only put text on your own clipboard.

Even so, do not paste real patient data unless your organization allows it.
